## ☀️ Thermal Physiology: Running in Menorca's Heat & Humidity

> **Athlete Note**: *"was my saturday GA run, but in menorca with much higher humidity and temperature"*

Running in high ambient temperatures combined with Mediterranean humidity creates specific physiological demands:

1. **Cutaneous Vasodilation & Cardiovascular Drift**:
   - In humid heat, sweat cannot evaporate efficiently from the skin. 
   - To cool down, your body redirects up to 15–20% of cardiac output away from leg muscles and toward the skin surface (*cutaneous vasodilation*).
   - This reduces venous return to the heart, causing your heart rate to run **8 to 15 bpm higher** at the same running pace compared to cool UK weather.
2. **Pacing Discipline (5:44 min/km)**:
   - Instead of stubbornly forcing 5:30/km, you naturally throttled back to **5:44 min/km**.
   - Because you eased back the pace, your average heart rate remained capped at **144.1 bpm**—meaning your cardiovascular system remained in the true **General Aerobic stimulus zone** despite the heat and +81m of climbing.
3. **Hydration Breaks (9m 12s paused)**:
   - Taking 9 minutes of total pauses for shade, hydration, and route checks prevented core body temperature from spiking.
