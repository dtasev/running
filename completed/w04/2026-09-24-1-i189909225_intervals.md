## 🔬 The Post-Viral Cardiac Signature: Why HR Was 150 bpm

Compare this run to your pre-illness baseline:
- **Week 3 Wednesday MLR (Sep 16)**: 5:26/km pace $\to$ **Avg HR: 139.7 bpm**
- **Week 4 Thursday (Today - Run 1)**: 5:29/km pace $\to$ **Avg HR: 150.1 bpm** (+10.4 bpm higher at the exact same pace)

### What causes this ~10 bpm elevation after illness?
1. **Temporary Plasma Volume Depletion**:
   - When fighting a virus (especially with low-grade fever or increased metabolic rate), plasma volume contracts by 4–8%.
   - With slightly less blood volume returning to the heart per beat (*reduced venous return/stroke volume*), the heart must beat ~8–12 bpm faster to deliver the exact same cardiac output to your legs.
2. **Elevated Sympathetic (Adrenergic) Tone**:
   - The immune battle elevates basal sympathetic nervous system activity and circulating catecholamines for 3 to 7 days post-infection.
3. **The Good News**:
   - This is **not a loss of aerobic fitness**! Your mitochondrial density and capillary networks built in Weeks 1–3 did not disappear in 3 days.
   - As hydration normalizes and the immune system fully powers down, your heart rate will drop right back to ~140 bpm within 3–5 days.
