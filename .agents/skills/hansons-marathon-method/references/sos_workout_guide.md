# Hansons Something of Substance (SOS) Workout Guide

This reference details the exact workout progressions for Speed, Strength, and Tempo runs in the Hansons system.

---

## 1. Speed Workouts (Weeks 1–9)
All Speed workouts include a 1.5 to 2.5-mile warm-up and cool-down. Recovery intervals are 400m jog (approx. half the duration of the rep).

- **Week 1**: $12 \times 400\text{m @ 5k pace}$ (400m jog)
- **Week 2**: $8 \times 600\text{m @ 5k pace}$ (400m jog)
- **Week 3**: $6 \times 800\text{m @ 5k-10k pace}$ (400m jog)
- **Week 4**: $5 \times 1000\text{m @ 5k-10k pace}$ (400m jog)
- **Week 5**: $4 \times 1200\text{m @ 10k pace}$ (400m jog)
- **Week 6**: $3 \times 1600\text{m @ 10k pace}$ (400m jog)
- **Week 7**: $12 \times 400\text{m @ 5k pace}$ (400m jog)
- **Week 8**: $8 \times 600\text{m @ 5k pace}$ (400m jog)
- **Week 9**: $6 \times 800\text{m @ 5k-10k pace}$ (400m jog)

---

## 2. Strength Workouts (Weeks 10–18)
Pace is **10 seconds per mile faster than Marathon Goal Pace (MGP - 10s/mi)**. Recovery intervals are 400m–800m jog.

- **Week 10**: $6 \times 1\text{ mile @ (MGP - 10s)}$ (400m recovery jog)
- **Week 11**: $4 \times 1.5\text{ miles @ (MGP - 10s)}$ (600m recovery jog)
- **Week 12**: $3 \times 2\text{ miles @ (MGP - 10s)}$ (800m recovery jog)
- **Week 13**: $2 \times 3\text{ miles @ (MGP - 10s)}$ (800m recovery jog)
- **Week 14**: $6 \times 1\text{ mile @ (MGP - 10s)}$ (400m recovery jog)
- **Week 15**: $4 \times 1.5\text{ miles @ (MGP - 10s)}$ (600m recovery jog)
- **Week 16**: $3 \times 2\text{ miles @ (MGP - 10s)}$ (800m recovery jog)
- **Week 17**: $2 \times 2\text{ miles @ (MGP - 10s)}$ (800m recovery jog)
- **Week 18 (Race Week)**: $3 \times 1\text{ mile @ (MGP - 10s)}$ (400m jog) on Tuesday.

---

## 3. Tempo Runs (Weeks 1–18)
All Tempo runs are run at **EXACT Goal Marathon Pace (MGP)** with 1.5–2.5 miles warm-up and cool-down.

- **Weeks 1–5**: 5 to 6 miles @ MGP
- **Weeks 6–8**: 7 to 8 miles @ MGP
- **Weeks 9–11**: 8 to 9 miles @ MGP
- **Weeks 12–15**: 10 miles @ MGP
- **Week 16**: 8 miles @ MGP
- **Week 17**: 6 miles @ MGP
- **Week 18 (Race Week)**: 4 miles @ MGP on Thursday.

---

## 4. Long Run Schedule
Long runs are run at **MGP + 30 to 90 seconds per mile**:
- Alternates 10 miles, 12 miles, 14 miles, 15 miles, and 16 miles.
- Capped strictly at **16 miles (25.7 km)**.
