# Magic Mile Protocol and Galloway Race Day Strategy

This reference details the Magic Mile time trial testing protocol and race-day pacing strategies.

---

## 1. Magic Mile (MM) Protocol

- **Frequency**: Every 2–3 weeks on alternating weekends.
- **Warm-up**: 1 mile easy jog + 4 strides.
- **Testing**: Run 1 mile continuously or with minimal walk breaks at an evenly paced, hard effort (RPE 8.5/10).
- **Cool-down**: 1 mile easy jog.

---

## 2. Race-Day Execution Plan

1. **Start in the Correct Wave**: Line up according to your target pace, factoring in your planned Run-Walk-Run interval.
2. **First Walk Break at 0.5–1.0 Minutes**: Take your very first walk break within the first 60–90 seconds of the race. Do not skip early walk breaks because you feel fresh.
3. **Crowd Management**: Move slightly to the right side of the road or raise your hand before shifting to a walk break so runners behind you can easily pass.
4. **Water Stations**: Walk through every water station to drink and ingest fuel calmly without spilling.
5. **The Final 10k**: While other runners hit the wall and slow to 15–18 min/mile, Galloway runners maintain consistent average pace and regularly pass hundreds of competitors in the final 6 miles.
