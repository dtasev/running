## 2. Walking Technique Rules

1. **Posture**: Maintain tall posture during the walk break with hands relaxed near hips; do not slouch or lean backward.
2. **Cadence**: Walk briskly with short, quick steps rather than long, slow strides.
3. **Transition**: Gently glide from the running stride into the walk, rather than slamming on the brakes.
4. **Beeps / Timers**: Use a vibrating interval watch (Gymboss, Garmin Run/Walk alert) to keep timing automatic.
