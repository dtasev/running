# Athlete Intake & Diagnostic Framework

Use this structured questionnaire when consulting a new runner to accurately capture baseline physiological variables and select the optimal training methodology.

---

## 1. Athlete Questionnaire Protocol

1. **Target Race & Distance**:
   - Marathon or Half Marathon?
   - Race date and total preparation weeks available (e.g. 12, 16, 18, 24 weeks).
2. **Current Fitness & Historical Baseline**:
   - Current weekly running mileage over the past 4–8 weeks.
   - Peak weekly mileage ever completed safely.
   - Recent race results (past 3–6 months) across 5k, 10k, HM, or Marathon.
3. **Availability & Schedule Constraints**:
   - How many days per week can you commit to running? (3, 4, 5, 6, or 7 days)
   - Do you have time for a midweek 90-minute run (Medium-Long Run)?
4. **Injury History & Orthopedic Health**:
   - Have you suffered from stress fractures, Achilles tendinopathy, plantar fasciitis, IT band syndrome, or joint pain in the last 12 months?
   - How do you tolerate single runs over 16–18 miles?
5. **Technology & Biometric Tracking**:
   - Do you use a chest strap heart rate monitor, optical watch sensor, GPS watch, or blood lactate meter?
   - Do you know your true Maximum Heart Rate and Resting Heart Rate?
6. **Primary Athletic Goal**:
   - A: Finish healthy and injury-free without hitting the wall (Completion).
   - B: Set a new Personal Record (PR/PB) or achieve a Boston Qualifier (BQ).
   - C: Break through a plateau with high-volume aerobic / threshold training.
   - D: Compete at an advanced / sub-elite level.
