# Running Methodology Comparison Matrix & Selection Framework

This master reference provides an exhaustive side-by-side comparison of the 7 running training methodologies for Half Marathon and Marathon coaching.

---

## 1. Comprehensive Comparison Matrix

| Attribute | Jack Daniels (VDOT) | Pete Pfitzinger (Pfitz) | Hansons Marathon Method | Norwegian Endurance | Renato Canova | Phil Maffetone (MAF) | Jeff Galloway (RWR) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Metric** | VDOT & Race Velocity | % Max HR / % HRR & LT Pace | Marathon Goal Pace (MGP) | Blood Lactate (mmol/L) & HR | % of Race Pace (Specific MP) | MAF HR (180 Formula) | Run/Walk Time Ratio |
| **Key Distinctive Workout** | Cruise Intervals ($5 \times 1\text{ mi @ T}$) & 2Q Long Runs | Midweek Medium-Long Run (11–15 mi) & LT Tempos | 3 Something of Substance (SOS) sessions weekly | Double Threshold days ($20 \times 400\text{m} / 5 \times 6\text{ min}$) | Special Blocks (AM/PM double hard sessions) | Monthly MAF 5-mile track test | Magic Mile time trial & Over-distance long runs |
| **Long Run Strategy** | Quality embedded (up to 18–20 mi or 2.5 hrs) | Progressive to +10% MP (up to 20–22 mi) | **Capped at 16 miles** (or 2.5–3 hrs) | Specific MP blocks (e.g. $3 \times 6\text{ km @ MP}$) | Long Fast Runs (28–35 km @ 92–95% MP) | Easy conversational HR (up to 20 mi) | Over-distance (up to 26–29 mi with walk breaks) |
| **Weekly Volume Range** | 30 to 100+ miles/week | 45 to 85+ miles/week | 40 to 65 miles/week | 50 to 110+ miles/week | 75 to 140+ miles/week | 25 to 70 miles/week | 15 to 35 miles/week |
| **Days / Week Running** | 4 to 6 days | 5 to 6 days | **Strictly 6 days** | 5 to 7 days | 6 to 7 days | 4 to 6 days | 3 to 4 days |
| **Injury Risk Profile** | Moderate | Moderate-High | Moderate | Low-Moderate | High (Elite only) | Very Low | Minimal |
| **Best Target Audience** | Data-driven runners, flexible schedules | Ambitious runners seeking BQ/PR | Runners who struggle with 20+ miler recovery | Runners with HR/lactate sensors | Competitive / Sub-elite / Elite | Base-building, overtrained runners | Beginners, injury-prone, master runners |
