# Pfitzinger Mesocycle Periodization and Race Preparation Guide

This reference details the 4 mesocycles, progression logic, and tune-up race integration in Pfitzinger & Douglas marathon and half-marathon plans.

---

## 1. Mesocycle Breakdown

### Mesocycle 1: Endurance (Weeks 18–13 or 12–10)
- **Primary Goal**: Build continuous aerobic volume, establish routine midweek Medium-Long Runs (MLRs), and reinforce connective tissue strength.
- **Key Sessions**:
  - Weekly Long Run starting at 13–15 miles.
  - Weekly Midweek MLR of 10–12 miles.
  - General Aerobic runs with $6-10 \times 100\text{m}$ strides for neuromuscular turnover.
  - Intro LT tempo (e.g., 4 miles @ LT).

### Mesocycle 2: Lactate Threshold & Endurance (Weeks 12–7 or 9–6)
- **Primary Goal**: Elevate the aerobic threshold and condition the body to burn fat at race pace.
- **Key Sessions**:
  - Weekly or bi-weekly Lactate Threshold tempos (5, 6, and 7 miles @ LT).
  - Marathon Pace long runs (e.g., 16 mi with 10 mi @ MP, 18 mi with 14 mi @ MP).
  - Peak MLR volume (12–15 miles).
  - Over-distance long runs (18–20 miles).

### Mesocycle 3: Race Preparation & Sharpening (Weeks 6–3 or 5–3)
- **Primary Goal**: Peak volume, VO2max sharpening, race-day simulation, and tune-up racing.
- **Key Sessions**:
  - Peak long runs: 20–22 miles (32–35 km).
  - VO2max workouts: $5 \times 600\text{m}$, $5 \times 1000\text{m}$, or $6 \times 800\text{m} \text{ @ 5k pace}$.
  - Two to three Tune-Up Races (8k to 15k).

### Mesocycle 4: Taper & Peak (Final 3 Weeks)
- **Primary Goal**: Glycogen supercompensation, neuromuscular restoration, soft tissue recovery, and psychological freshness.
- **Volume Reduction**:
  - Week 3 before race: ~80% of peak volume.
  - Week 2 before race: ~60% of peak volume.
  - Race week: ~40% of peak volume (excluding the 26.2 mi race itself).
- **Intensity Touchpoints**: Maintain short LT or MP bursts ($2 \times 1\text{ mi @ LT}$ or $2\text{ mi @ MP}$) to maintain muscular tension without fatigue.

---

## 2. Pfitzinger Tune-Up Race Protocol

Pfitz incorporates **8k to 15k tune-up races** during Weeks 6, 4, and 2 before marathon race day:
1. **Schedule**:
   - Friday: 4–5 miles easy recovery.
   - Saturday: 8k to 15k Tune-Up Race (100% all-out effort to establish true race fitness and adjust marathon goal pace).
   - Sunday: Long Run of 16–18 miles at relaxed endurance pace.
2. **Value**:
   - Practicing pre-race meal timing, morning bowel routines, racing shoes, adrenaline control, and hydration stations.
   - Running the Sunday long run on pre-fatigued legs mimics the final 10 miles of a marathon safely.
