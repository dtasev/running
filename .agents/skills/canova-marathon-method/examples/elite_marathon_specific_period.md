# Renato Canova Elite Marathon Specific Period (8-Week Peak Microcycle)

This 8-week schedule demonstrates how Canova structures the peak phase for sub-elite and competitive marathoners targeting peak specific endurance.

---

## 8-Week Specific Period Schedule

| Week | Phase Focus | Key Midweek Session | Key Weekend Session | Total km (miles) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **8** | Special Endurance | $5 \times 3000\text{m @ 102% MP}$ (1000m float) | $32\text{ km Long Fast Run @ 92% MP}$ | 160 km (100 mi) |
| **7** | Specific Extension | $6 \times 2000\text{m @ 103% MP}$ (1000m float) | $4 \times 5000\text{m @ 100% MP}$ (1000m float) | 165 km (103 mi) |
| **6** | **Special Block Week** | **Special Block Day**:<br/>AM: 10k Easy + 10k Steady + 10k MP<br/>PM: 10k Easy + 5x2k @ 102% MP | $25\text{ km Moderate Aerobic Run}$ | 175 km (109 mi) |
| **5** | Specific Recovery | $10 \times 1000\text{m @ 105% MP}$ (HMP, 60s rest) | $30\text{ km Long Run (Final 10 km @ MP)}$ | 145 km (90 mi) |
| **4** | Specific Peak Volume | $3 \times 7000\text{m @ 99-101% MP}$ (1000m float) | $35\text{ km Long Fast Run @ 94% MP}$ | 170 km (106 mi) |
| **3** | Specific Sharpening | $4 \times 3000\text{m @ 101% MP}$ (1000m float) | $24\text{ km with 14 km @ MP}$ | 140 km (87 mi) |
| **2** | Taper 1 | $5 \times 1000\text{m @ 103% MP}$ (400m jog) | $18\text{ km with 8 km @ MP}$ | 110 km (68 mi) |
| **1** | Race Week | $3 \times 1000\text{m @ MP}$ (400m jog) | **MARATHON RACE DAY (42.195 km)** | 50 km + Race |
