# Canova Specific Blocks and Specific Workout Catalog

This reference outlines Renato Canova's most famous specific marathon workouts and special block configurations.

---

## 1. Specific Marathon Workouts

1. **The $4 \times 5000\text{m}$ Marathon Pace Simulation**:
   - $3\text{ km Warm-up}$
   - $4 \times 5000\text{m @ 98-101% MP}$ with $1000\text{m float @ 85% MP}$ (not a slow jog; keeps the runner moving at ~85% speed).
   - Total Volume: $27\text{ km}$ at very high average speed.
2. **The $3 \times 7000\text{m}$ Specific Endurance**:
   - $3 \times 7000\text{m @ 98-100% MP}$ with $1000\text{m float @ 80% MP}$.
   - Total Volume: $24\text{ km}$ continuous work.
3. **The Continuous Fast Run (Longo Veloce)**:
   - $30\text{ to }35\text{ km}$ at a continuous, steady pace of **92% to 95% of Marathon Pace**.

---

## 2. Special Block Architecture

A Special Block consists of two intensive training sessions on the exact same calendar day, executed once every 3–4 weeks during the Specific Period.

### Special Block 1: Aerobic & Specific Endurance
- **Morning (AM)**:
  - $10\text{ km Easy} + 10\text{ km Steady (88% MP)} + 10\text{ km Specific (100% MP)}$.
- **Afternoon (PM)**:
  - $10\text{ km Easy} + 10 \times 1000\text{m @ 103% MP}$ (HMP) with $1000\text{m float (85% MP)}$.

### Special Block 2: Intensive Specific Marathon Pace
- **Morning (AM)**:
  - $10\text{ km Easy} + 4 \times 3000\text{m @ 101% MP}$ (1000m float).
- **Afternoon (PM)**:
  - $10\text{ km Easy} + 6 \times 2000\text{m @ 102% MP}$ (1000m float).

---

## 3. Crucial Recovery Protocol Post-Special Block
- Day 1: 45 min very easy regeneration ($< 65\%$).
- Day 2: 50 min very easy regeneration ($< 65\%$) + light strides.
- Day 3: Standard aerobic base running (70–75%).
- No high-intensity workouts for at least 5 to 6 days after a Special Block.
