# Canova Funnel Periodization Framework

This guide details Renato Canova's progressive funnel periodization structure from General to Specific periods.

---

## 1. The Four Periods of Training

```text
[ General Period ]     --> High general volume + Max short sprint power (Hills 60-100m)
        |
[ Fundamental Period ] --> Aerobic capacity (80-90% MP) + Basic Threshold (105-110% MP)
        |
[ Special Period ]     --> Special Endurance (90-95% MP) + Intensive Speed (102-105% MP)
        |
[ Specific Period ]    --> Specific Marathon Volume (95-102% MP) + Special Blocks
```

---

## 2. Transition Rules Between Periods

### General Period (6–8 Weeks)
- **Aim**: Muscular recruitment, tendon stiffness, capillary base.
- **Key Workouts**: Continuous easy running, $10 \times 80\text{m}$ max hill sprints with full recovery, circuit resistance training.

### Fundamental Period (6–8 Weeks)
- **Aim**: Developing the aerobic engine to support marathon training.
- **Key Workouts**: Steady runs of 20–25 km, fartleks ($10 \times 2\text{ min fast} / 2\text{ min moderate}$), continuous uphill runs (5–8 km).

### Special Period (4–6 Weeks)
- **Aim**: Closing the gap toward race speed.
- **Key Workouts**: Long fast runs of 24–28 km @ 90-92% MP; Interval reps of $6 \times 2000\text{m @ 105% MP}$ (Half Marathon pace).

### Specific Period (Final 6–8 Weeks)
- **Aim**: Extending the duration a runner can hold 98–102% of Marathon Pace.
- **Key Workouts**: Long fast runs up to 35 km @ 95% MP; Specific intervals of $4 \times 5\text{ km @ MP}$; Special Blocks.
