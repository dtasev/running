# Lactate Testing and Heart Rate Zone Calibration

This reference explains how to calibrate and monitor training intensity without and with a portable lactate meter (e.g. Lactate Pro 2 / Edge).

---

## 1. Blood Lactate Sampling Protocol

- **Timing**: Take blood droplet sample 45–60 seconds into the rest interval after reps 2, 4, and the final rep.
- **Target Ranges**:
  - **Zone 1 (Easy)**: $0.8 - 1.5\text{ mmol/L}$
  - **Aerobic Threshold (LT1)**: $1.5 - 2.2\text{ mmol/L}$
  - **Lower Sub-Threshold (AM sessions)**: $2.0 - 2.8\text{ mmol/L}$
  - **Upper Sub-Threshold (PM sessions)**: $3.0 - 3.8\text{ mmol/L}$
  - **Lactate Threshold (LT2 / OBLA)**: $4.0\text{ mmol/L}$

---

## 2. Heart Rate Calibration (When Lactate Meter is Unavailable)

For runners without a lactate analyzer, heart rate percentages and perceived exertion serve as reliable proxies:

| Zone | % Max HR | % HRR | RPE (1-10) | Breathing Characteristic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Easy Aerobic (Zone 1)** | 65% - 75% | 60% - 70% | 2 - 3 | Full conversational, nasal breathing possible |
| **Lower Sub-Threshold (LT1+)** | 82% - 86% | 75% - 80% | 5 - 6 | 3-4 word sentences, rhythmical 3:3 or 2:2 breathing |
| **Upper Sub-Threshold (LT2-)** | 86% - 90% | 80% - 85% | 7 - 7.5 | 1-2 words only, comfortably hard, perfectly controlled |
| **Above Threshold (Avoided in DT)** | > 92% | > 88% | 8.5 - 10 | Hyperventilation, rapid burning in quads/calves |
