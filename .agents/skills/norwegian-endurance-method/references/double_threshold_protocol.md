# Double Threshold Execution Protocol (Norwegian Model)

This guide provides operational rules for executing Double Threshold (DT) training days safely and effectively.

---

## 1. The Chemistry of Double Threshold

When endurance athletes perform two sub-threshold workouts separated by 6 to 8 hours:
1. **Mitochondrial Signal**: The transcriptional coactivator PGC-1$\alpha$ is stimulated twice within the same 24-hour circadian window, multiplying enzyme synthesis.
2. **Substrate Efficiency**: The afternoon session begins with reduced muscle glycogen, forcing neuromuscular efficiency and fat utilization at elevated threshold speeds.
3. **Absence of Muscle Acid Damage**: Because lactate is capped at $< 3.8\text{ mmol/L}$, muscle intracellular pH remains neutral, eliminating the heavy delayed-onset muscle soreness (DOMS) typical of anaerobic $\text{VO}_2\text{max}$ intervals.

---

## 2. Typical Double Threshold Day Schedule

- **07:00 AM - Session 1 (Lower Threshold / Long Reps)**:
  - Warm-up: 2.5 km easy jog + dynamic mobility + 3 gentle strides.
  - Main Set: $5 \times 6\text{ minutes}$ (or $4 \times 2\text{ km}$) @ $2.0 - 2.5\text{ mmol/L}$ (Half Marathon pace) with 60s standing/light walking rest.
  - Cool-down: 2 km easy jog.
  - Post-run: Immediate carbohydrate + protein intake (60g carbs, 25g protein) + rehydration.
- **12:00 PM - Lunch & Rest**:
  - High-complex-carb meal, 30–45 minute nap or passive relaxation.
- **16:30 PM - Session 2 (Upper Threshold / Short Reps)**:
  - Warm-up: 2.5 km easy jog + 2 strides.
  - Main Set: $20-25 \times 400\text{m}$ (or $10 \times 1\text{ km}$) @ $3.0 - 3.6\text{ mmol/L}$ (10k pace) with 30s rest (for 400s) or 45-60s rest (for 1k).
  - Cool-down: 2 km easy jog.
  - Post-run: Evening nutrient-dense recovery meal.

---

## 3. Critical Rules for Non-Elite / Amateur Runners

1. **Start with Single Threshold Days**: Athletes should log at least 6 months of weekly single threshold sessions before attempting double threshold.
2. **Cap DT Days to Once Weekly**: For marathoners running 50–70 miles/week, one Double Threshold day (e.g. Tuesday) + one long run workout (Sunday) is the optimal ceiling.
3. **Honor the Speed Limit**: The single biggest mistake is running the intervals at 5k race pace. If lactate creeps over 4.0 mmol/L, the autonomic system is overtaxed, ruining the following days of training.
