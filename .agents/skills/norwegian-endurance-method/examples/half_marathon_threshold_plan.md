# Norwegian Model Half Marathon 12-Week Training Plan

A dedicated half-marathon plan leveraging sub-threshold volume and short recovery intervals to maximize threshold velocity.

---

## 12-Week Schedule Overview

| Week | Target Volume | Tuesday (Threshold 1) | Thursday (Threshold 2) | Weekend Long Run |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **1** | 40 mi (64k) | $5 \times 6\text{ min @ } 2.2\text{ mmol/L}$ (60s rest) | $15 \times 400\text{m @ } 3.2\text{ mmol/L}$ (30s rest) | 10 mi (16k) Zone 1 Easy |
| **2** | 44 mi (70k) | $4 \times 2\text{ km @ } 2.4\text{ mmol/L}$ (60s rest) | $8 \times 1\text{ km @ } 3.4\text{ mmol/L}$ (45s rest) | 11 mi (17.5k) Steady |
| **3** | 48 mi (77k) | **DT Day**: AM: $5 \times 6\text{ min}$; PM: $20 \times 400\text{m}$ | 45 min Zone 1 Easy | 12 mi (19k) with $4 \times 2\text{ km @ HM Pace}$ |
| **4** | 36 mi (58k) | $4 \times 1.5\text{ km @ } 2.2\text{ mmol/L}$ (60s rest) | 40 min Zone 1 Easy | 9 mi (14.5k) Recovery Week |
| **5** | 50 mi (80k) | $5 \times 2\text{ km @ } 2.5\text{ mmol/L}$ (60s rest) | $10 \times 1\text{ km @ } 3.5\text{ mmol/L}$ (45s rest) | 13 mi (21k) Steady |
| **6** | 54 mi (86k) | **DT Day**: AM: $4 \times 2\text{ km}$; PM: $12 \times 1\text{ km}$ | 45 min Zone 1 Easy | 13 mi with $8\text{ km @ HM Pace}$ |
| **7** | 56 mi (90k) | $3 \times 3\text{ km @ } 2.5\text{ mmol/L}$ (90s rest) | $20 \times 400\text{m @ } 3.5\text{ mmol/L}$ (30s rest) | 14 mi (22.5k) Long Run |
| **8** | 42 mi (67k) | $4 \times 2\text{ km @ } 2.2\text{ mmol/L}$ (60s rest) | 40 min Zone 1 Easy | 5k or 10k Tune-up Race |
| **9** | 58 mi (93k) | **DT Day**: AM: $5 \times 6\text{ min}$; PM: $10 \times 1\text{ km}$ | $4 \times 2\text{ km @ } 2.8\text{ mmol/L}$ | 14 mi with $10\text{ km @ HM Pace}$ |
| **10**| 55 mi (88k) | $4 \times 2.5\text{ km @ } 2.6\text{ mmol/L}$ (75s rest)| $15 \times 400\text{m @ } 3.5\text{ mmol/L}$ (30s rest) | 12 mi (19k) Steady |
| **11**| 40 mi (64k) | $4 \times 1.5\text{ km @ } 2.4\text{ mmol/L}$ (60s rest)| 35 min Zone 1 Easy | 8 mi (13k) Taper |
| **12**| 18 mi + Race | $8 \times 400\text{m @ } 3.2\text{ mmol/L}$ (30s rest) | 20 min Easy Jog + 3 Strides | **HALF MARATHON (21.1 km)** |
