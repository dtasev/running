# Jack Daniels 18-Week 2Q Marathon Training Plan (Up to 55-70 Miles / 90-112 km Peak)

This plan follows Jack Daniels' Two Quality (2Q) approach. Two specific quality sessions (Q1 and Q2) are performed each week, with the remaining days filled with Easy (E) running to reach target weekly volume.

---

## Weekly Structure Overview
- **Tuesday / Wednesday**: Q1 (Interval, Threshold, or mixed workout)
- **Sunday**: Q2 (Long Run with specific M or T components)
- **Monday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday**: Easy (E) aerobic runs + 1 Rest / Cross-train day.

---
