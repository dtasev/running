# Jack Daniels 16-Week Half Marathon Training Plan

This plan emphasizes lactate threshold expansion and aerobic speed endurance, the two primary physiological determinants of half-marathon success.

---

## Weekly Structure
- **Wednesday**: Quality 1 (Cruise Intervals, Threshold Tempo, or VO2max intervals)
- **Saturday / Sunday**: Quality 2 (Long Run, often with fast-finish T or M blocks)
- **Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday**: Easy recovery runs (E-pace) + strides.

---

## 16-Week Schedule Overview

| Week | Target Volume | Midweek Quality (Q1) | Weekend Quality (Q2) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **16** | 30 mi (48 km) | $2\text{ mi E} + 6 \times 200\text{m R} (200\text{m jog}) + 2\text{ mi E}$ | $8\text{ mi (13 km) E}$ |
| **15** | 35 mi (56 km) | $2\text{ mi E} + 8 \times 200\text{m R} (200\text{m jog}) + 2\text{ mi E}$ | $9\text{ mi (14.5 km) E}$ |
| **14** | 38 mi (61 km) | $2\text{ mi E} + 3 \times 1\text{ mi T (1 min rest)} + 2\text{ mi E}$ | $10\text{ mi (16 km) E with 6 strides}$ |
| **13** | 40 mi (64 km) | $2\text{ mi E} + 4 \times 1\text{ mi T (1 min rest)} + 2\text{ mi E}$ | $10\text{ mi: } 7\text{ mi E} + 3\text{ mi @ HM Goal Pace}$ |
| **12** | 32 mi (51 km) | $2\text{ mi E} + 5 \times 400\text{m R (400m jog)} + 2\text{ mi E}$ | $8\text{ mi (13 km) E}$ (**Recovery Week**) |
| **11** | 42 mi (67 km) | $2\text{ mi E} + 5 \times 1000\text{m I (3 min jog)} + 2\text{ mi E}$ | $11\text{ mi (17.5 km) E}$ |
| **10** | 45 mi (72 km) | $2\text{ mi E} + 4 \times 1200\text{m I (3 min jog)} + 2\text{ mi E}$ | $12\text{ mi: } 8\text{ mi E} + 4\text{ mi @ HM Pace}$ |
| **9** | 46 mi (74 km) | $2\text{ mi E} + 5 \times 1\text{ mi T (1 min rest)} + 2\text{ mi E}$ | $12\text{ mi (19 km) E}$ |
| **8** | 35 mi (56 km) | $2\text{ mi E} + 3 \times 1\text{ mi T} + 4 \times 200\text{m R} + 1\text{ mi E}$ | $5k \text{ or } 10k \text{ Tune-up Race}$ |
| **7** | 48 mi (77 km) | $2\text{ mi E} + 3 \times 2\text{ mi T (2 min rest)} + 2\text{ mi E}$ | $13\text{ mi (21 km) E}$ |
| **6** | 50 mi (80 km) | $2\text{ mi E} + 4 \times 1.5\text{ mi T (2 min rest)} + 2\text{ mi E}$ | $13\text{ mi: } 7\text{ mi E} + 5\text{ mi @ HM Pace} + 1\text{ mi E}$ |
| **5** | 50 mi (80 km) | $2\text{ mi E} + 6 \times 1\text{ mi T (1 min rest)} + 2\text{ mi E}$ | $14\text{ mi (22.5 km) E steady}$ |
| **4** | 45 mi (72 km) | $2\text{ mi E} + 4 \times 1\text{ mi T (1 min rest)} + 4 \times 200\text{ R}$ | $12\text{ mi (19 km) E}$ |
| **3** | 38 mi (61 km) | $2\text{ mi E} + 3 \times 1\text{ mi T (1 min rest)} + 2\text{ mi E}$ | $10\text{ mi: } 6\text{ mi E} + 3\text{ mi @ HM Pace} + 1\text{ mi E}$ |
| **2** | 30 mi (48 km) | $2\text{ mi E} + 2 \times 1\text{ mi T (1 min rest)} + 4 \times 200\text{ R}$ | $8\text{ mi (13 km) E with strides}$ (**Taper**) |
| **1** | 18 mi + Race | $2\text{ mi E} + 3 \times 1\text{ km @ HM Pace (2 min rest)} + 1\text{ mi E}$ | **HALF MARATHON RACE DAY: 13.1 mi (21.1 km)** |
