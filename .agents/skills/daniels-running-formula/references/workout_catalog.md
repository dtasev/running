# Jack Daniels Complete Workout Catalog

This catalog outlines standard workouts across all intensity zones for Half Marathon and Marathon athletes.

---

## 1. Easy & Long Runs (E / L)

- **Standard Aerobic Run**:
  - Distance: 45–75 minutes at E-pace.
  - Purpose: Capillary and mitochondrial adaptation, recovery between hard sessions.
- **Strides Integration**:
  - Format: Easy run followed by $6 \times 20\text{s}$ fast, relaxed accelerations (90s walk recovery).
  - Purpose: Running economy, leg turnover without lactate accumulation.
- **Classic Long Run (L)**:
  - Distance: 90–150 minutes (max 2.5 hours or 25-30% of weekly volume, whichever comes first).
  - Purpose: Glycogen conservation, psychological stamina, musculoskeletal resilience.

---

## 2. Marathon Pace Workouts (M)

- **M-Pace Progression**:
  - Format: $2\text{ mi E} + 8\text{ to }14\text{ mi @ M} + 1\text{ mi E}$.
  - Purpose: Practicing target race rhythm, checking hydration/gel tolerance at race pace.
- **Alternating M / E Long Run**:
  - Format: $16\text{ mi total}$: alternating $2\text{ mi E} / 2\text{ mi @ M}$ throughout.
  - Purpose: Teaches the body to clear lactate and recover at relatively fast aerobic speeds.

---

## 3. Threshold (T) Workouts (Lactate Threshold)

- **Steady Tempo Run**:
  - Format: $2\text{ mi E warm-up} + 20\text{ to }40\text{ min @ T} + 2\text{ mi E cool-down}$.
  - Guideline: 20 min @ exact T-pace; for 30–40 min, Daniels adjusts pace slightly slower (+3 to +5 sec/km).
- **Cruise Intervals (The Daniels Signature)**:
  - Format 1: $5 \times 1\text{ mile @ T}$ with 1:00 min recovery jog.
  - Format 2: $4 \times 2\text{ km @ T}$ with 90s recovery jog.
  - Format 3: $3 \times 2\text{ miles @ T}$ with 2:00 min recovery jog.
  - Rule: Recovery time should be roughly $1/5\text{th}$ of the work bout duration (5:1 work-to-rest ratio).

---

## 4. Interval (I) Workouts ($\text{VO}_2\text{max}$)

- **Classic $1000\text{m}$ Repeats**:
  - Format: $2\text{ mi E warm-up} + 5 \times 1000\text{m @ I}$ with 3:00 min jog recovery $+ 2\text{ mi E cool-down}$.
- **$1200\text{m} / 800\text{m}$ Ladder**:
  - Format: $1200\text{m} - 1000\text{m} - 800\text{m} - 1000\text{m} - 1200\text{m} \text{ @ I}$ with 1:1 work-to-rest jog.
- **Rule of Interval Volume**: Total hard volume at I-pace must not exceed 8% of weekly volume or $10\text{ km}$, whichever is less.

---

## 5. Repetition (R) Workouts (Economy & Speed)

- **$200\text{m} / 400\text{m}$ Economy Repeats**:
  - Format: $2\text{ mi E} + 8 \times 200\text{m @ R}$ (200m walk/jog) $+ 4 \times 400\text{m @ R}$ (400m walk/jog) $+ 1\text{ mi E}$.
- **Short Hill Repeats**:
  - Format: $10 \times 45\text{s}$ hill sprints at R-effort with walk-down full recovery.
- **Rule of Repetition Recovery**: Full recovery is mandatory so that mechanics do not deteriorate.

---

## 6. Combined Quality Workouts (2Q Specials)

- **T + M Hybrid**:
  - Format: $2\text{ mi E} + 2 \times 2\text{ mi @ T (2 min rest)} + 8\text{ mi @ M} + 1\text{ mi E}$.
- **I + T Fast Finish**:
  - Format: $2\text{ mi E} + 3 \times 1\text{ mi @ T (1 min rest)} + 4 \times 400\text{m @ I (90s rest)} + 1\text{ mi E}$.
