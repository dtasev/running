## 🏛️ Course-Specific Preparation for Rome Marathon
1. **Cobblestone Conditioning (*Sanpietrini*)**: The final 7 km and several city-center stretches feature Rome’s historic cobblestones. The strides and Sunday aerobic runs after Saturday's long run condition your lower legs and feet to absorb repetitive impact on tired muscles.
2. **Hydration & Fueling Targets**: Ingest **50–60g carbs/hour** (1 energy gel every 30 minutes with 120–150 ml water). Practice this exact fueling during your Saturday long runs starting in Week 12.
