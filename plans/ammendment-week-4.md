## 🩺 Post-Viral Physiological Guidelines

1. **Cardiac Lag**: Post-viral plasma volume recovery and elevated sympathetic tone typically cause heart rate to run 3–8 bpm higher at standard paces for 4–7 days. Let heart rate govern effort rather than rigidly enforcing pace.
2. **Hydration & Electrolytes**: Maintain higher fluid and electrolyte intake through Sunday to restore circulating plasma volume.
3. **The "Pull-the-Plug" Rule on Sunday's Long Run**: If elevated cardiac drift (> 155 bpm at easy pace) or heavy fatigue arises during Sunday's long run, cap the session at 12–14 km. Quality of recovery takes precedence over hitting arbitrary numbers.
